# Historical analysis preservation and five-completed-slate evaluation

## Immutable historical record

Preserve original pregame forecasts and minutes, original/normalized DARKO values when recorded, methodology actually used, opportunity evidence, source dates, ranges, assumptions, exact lines/provider/tier, rubric, original grade, selections, exclusions and failure scenarios. Never overwrite them with a later framework or postgame knowledge. Missing original fields remain missing.

Separate research targets, final research selections and confirmed wagers/entries. A recommendation is not evidence of placement. Do not combine these categories in performance calculations or infer ROI from research results.

Preserve evidence of when information was available and when it was actually reviewed; recovered statistics are not automatically contemporaneous evidence. Retain originals with checksums. Corrections and later annotations should be separately dated and linked to the original; retrospective reconstructions must be labeled. Track analytical decision time separately from import/recovery time and framework adoption time.

Review wins and losses using the same process standards. Examine minutes, role, ownership, opportunity volume, opportunity quality, conversion, matchup, script, market and original confidence; inspect combined-market components. Distinguish predictable errors, ordinary variance, unusual events and insufficient evidence. Do not infer chances or shot quality from box scores, double-count one loss as independent errors, or rank exclusions using hindsight.
